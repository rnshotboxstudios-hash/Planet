# Planet — year-four future concept

Requested visualization: one student's world after four years of upgrades, player and every agent at maximum level 100. These are proposed future designs, not screenshots of implemented city management, actual earnings, or a payment integration.

Three scales: full planet, district cutaway, and Design Works upper-floor room 07. All use the same proposed layout: five connected buildings, two floors per building, two productive rooms per floor, twenty rooms total. The central plaza command monument and exterior infrastructure do not add productive rooms.

| Building | Floor 1 | Floor 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moss-green Research HQ | 01 Mission intake; 02 Research questions | 03 Source review; 04 Opportunity planning |
| Teal Design Works | 05 Brand studio; 06 Product sketches | 07 Master design workshop; 08 Prototype testing |
| Copper Publishing Mill | 09 Content drafting; 10 Site assembly | 11 Product packaging; 12 Delivery queue |
| Violet Quality Lab | 13 Accuracy checks; 14 Accessibility checks | 15 Workflow maintenance; 16 Adult review |
| Gold Market Hall | 17 Store operations; 18 Customer-service drafts | 19 Order reconciliation; 20 Earnings overview |

The visualized business system includes orders, delivery, earnings review, and a proposed teen-wallet route. Real payments, account eligibility, guardian sponsorship, provider support, customer activity, and any actual income must be independently implemented and verified. No money balances or guaranteed earnings are represented.

Alien ecosystems remain outside rooms. Workers are original oversized-head anime collectible-style characters; art direction uses smooth octagonal voxel details, forest-green/gold game HUD, and clear separated text borders.
